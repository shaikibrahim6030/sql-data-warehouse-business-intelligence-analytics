# 🗄️ SQL Data Warehouse & Business Intelligence Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the complete lifecycle of modern Data Engineering and Data Analytics using SQL Server. The objective was to transform raw business data from multiple source systems into analytics-ready datasets and generate actionable business insights through Business Intelligence reporting and advanced SQL analytics.

The solution combines Data Warehousing, ETL Development, Data Modeling, Customer Analytics, Product Analytics, Sales Analytics, and KPI Reporting to simulate a real-world enterprise analytics environment. By integrating CRM and ERP datasets, the project provides a scalable foundation for reporting, performance monitoring, and data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations often store business data across multiple systems, making it difficult to generate consistent reports and actionable insights. Raw operational data is frequently incomplete, duplicated, and not optimized for analytical workloads.

The goal of this project was to:

* Consolidate data from multiple business systems.
* Improve data quality through cleansing and transformation.
* Build an analytics-ready Data Warehouse.
* Generate meaningful business insights through advanced SQL analytics.
* Support KPI monitoring and business reporting.

---

## 🏗️ What Was Implemented

### Data Warehouse Development

Designed and implemented a modern Data Warehouse using Medallion Architecture:

🥉 Bronze Layer – Raw data ingestion from source systems.

🥈 Silver Layer – Data cleansing, standardization, transformation, and quality improvements.

🥇 Gold Layer – Business-ready analytical datasets optimized for reporting and decision-making.

---

### ETL Pipeline Development

Developed ETL processes to:

* Extract data from CRM and ERP systems.
* Clean and validate business data.
* Standardize records and business entities.
* Transform raw data into structured analytical datasets.
* Load processed data into reporting-ready layers.

---

### Data Modeling

Built dimensional data models using:

* Fact Tables
* Dimension Tables
* Star Schema Design

The data model was optimized for fast analytical queries, reporting efficiency, and business intelligence applications.

---

### Business Intelligence & Analytics

Performed advanced SQL analytics to answer key business questions related to:

👥 Customer Performance

* Revenue contribution analysis
* Customer purchasing behavior
* High-value customer identification

🛍️ Product Performance

* Product sales analysis
* Category performance evaluation

📈 Sales Analytics
